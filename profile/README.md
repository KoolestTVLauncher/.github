**Koolest** is a free, open-source launcher for Android TV and Google TV. Your apps, your shows and your consoles on one home screen, with no ads, no account and no tracking.

- **Website:** [koolesttvlauncher.github.io](https://koolesttvlauncher.github.io) ([en français](https://koolesttvlauncher.github.io/fr/))
- **Download:** [Koolest.apk](https://github.com/KoolestTVLauncher/Koolest-TV-Launcher/releases/latest/download/Koolest.apk), or type `koolesttvlauncher.github.io/get` in the Downloader app on your TV
- **Source and issues:** [Koolest-TV-Launcher](https://github.com/KoolestTVLauncher/Koolest-TV-Launcher)
