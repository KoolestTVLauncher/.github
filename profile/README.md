**Koolest** is a free, open-source launcher for Android TV and Google TV. Your apps, your shows and your consoles on one home screen, with no ads, no account and no tracking.

- **Website:** [koolest.parsakp.com](https://koolest.parsakp.com) ([en français](https://koolest.parsakp.com/fr/), [به فارسی](https://koolest.parsakp.com/fa/))
- **Download:** [Koolest.apk](https://github.com/KoolestTVLauncher/Koolest-TV-Launcher/releases/latest/download/Koolest.apk), or type `koolest.parsakp.com/get` in the Downloader app on your TV
- **Source and issues:** [Koolest-TV-Launcher](https://github.com/KoolestTVLauncher/Koolest-TV-Launcher)
